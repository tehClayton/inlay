# inlay

Learn the fretboard of a stringed instrument: guitar, bass, banjo, ukulele,
mandolin, or anything you can describe by its strings, tuning and fret count.

inlay drills you on finding and naming notes, intervals, chord tones and scales,
times each answer, and shows where on the neck you are slow or unsure.

**Status:** early development, not yet usable.

No accounts, no tracking, no network. Everything you do stays on your device, and
you can export it to a backup file and import it again.

A companion to [millitap](https://github.com/tehClayton/millitap).
